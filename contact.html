FAQ

Before you write

How quickly will you reply?

We're a student team, so replies usually come within a few days, a little longer during busy competition weekends. Team leads answer with mentor oversight.

Can our robot visit my school or event?

We love doing demos. Choose "Book a demo" above and tell us the date, location, and what kind of event it is.

Who can join Steel Surge?

Students in grades 7–12 in our area. No experience is needed. Fill out the interest form or send us a message.

What is FTC?

FIRST Tech Challenge is a robotics program for grades 7–12. Our FTC page explains everything, including every recent game.
