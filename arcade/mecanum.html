How to play

  • Mecanum wheels let a robot drive in any direction, including sideways, without turning.
  • Drive through every gate in order, then cross the finish line. The next gate glows.
  • Touching a cone adds a 1-second penalty.
  • Beat the gold time for a gold medal. Your best run races alongside you as a ghost.
