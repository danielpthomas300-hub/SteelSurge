How to play

  • Every robot needs wiring. Connect the battery to every motor and sensor on the board.
  • Click or tap a tile to rotate it. Powered wires light up blue.
  • The puzzle is solved when every part has power. Every circuit is guaranteed to have a solution.
  • The difficulty setting picks the board size, from 4×4 on Super Easy to 11×11 on Extreme. On Hard and Extreme, wires can wrap around the edges.
