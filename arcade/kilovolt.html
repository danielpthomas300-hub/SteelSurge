How to play

  • Each round has 7 prompts. Five ask for a team, like “Name an FTC team from Ohio.” One asks about a past FTC game, like “Name a game element from SKYSTONE,” and one about FTC in general: awards, robot parts, sensors, code, and more.
  • For team prompts, type the name of a real FTC team that fits. Capitals, spaces, and plurals don't matter, and one typo is forgiven (two in long names) as long as the first letter is right. You get one answer per prompt, and the clock is running.
  • Any correct answer charges you up, but the less famous the team, the more it's worth: Spark (100 V), Static, Current, Voltage, Overload, and Full Surge (1,000 V).
  • Team fame is based on each team's awards, seasons, and events, using official data from FTCScout, and covers every FTC team since 2019–20. On the other prompts, the less obvious answers are worth more.
  • Everyone gets the same Daily Charge, once a day. Want more? Play Unlimited rounds as often as you like.
  • Round settings shape Unlimited rounds: 5 to 15 prompts or Endless (keep going until your third miss), teams only or FTC knowledge only, the topics you want, prompts that match your difficulty, no timer, and no repeats. Custom rounds don't count toward your best score.
  • Practice has no timer and no score, and shows every answer after each prompt, so it's a good way to study FTC history.
  • My stats shows your Daily Charge history: streaks, best and average days, the last 30 days, and how your answers spread across the tiers.
