How to play

  • Each round has 7 prompts, like “Name an FTC team from Ohio” or “Name an FTC team with an animal in its name.”
  • Type the name of a real FTC team that fits. Small typos are OK. You get one answer per prompt, and the clock is running.
  • Any correct answer charges you up, but the less famous the team, the more it's worth: Spark (100 V), Static, Current, Voltage, Overload, and Full Surge (1,000 V).
  • Fame is based on each team's awards, seasons, and events, using official data from FTCScout. Answers cover every FTC team that has competed since 2019–20.
  • Everyone gets the same Daily Charge, once a day. Want more? Play Unlimited rounds as often as you like.
