How to play

  • Just like our real robot, you launch balls with a spinning flywheel and an adjustable hood.
  • Set the hood angle and flywheel speed, then fire. Watch the wind meter: wind pushes your shot.
  • Land pollen in the hive to score. The glowing center ring is worth more.
  • Score shots in a row to build a streak multiplier. Each round gives you 10 pollen.
  • Targets start moving and the wind gets stronger as your score climbs.
