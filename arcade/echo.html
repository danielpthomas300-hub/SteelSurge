How to play

  • Each loop has a battery: a limited number of ticks. Every move or wait uses one.
  • Press Rewind and your loop becomes an echo, a ghost that replays exactly what you did, step for step.
  • Plates open doors of the same color while anyone (you or an echo) stands on them.
  • Red striped barriers do the opposite: they close while their plate is pressed.
  • Reach the green exit. Each level limits how many echoes you can keep.
