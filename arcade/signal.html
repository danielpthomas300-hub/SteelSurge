How to play

  • You're lost in a dark factory. Your robot can only see right around itself.
  • Press Ping to send out a pulse that briefly lights up the walls around you.
  • Every ping can be heard: security drones nearby turn and hunt the spot you pinged from.
  • Collect all the data cores, then reach the glowing exit to go down a floor.
  • In a tight spot? Drop a decoy. It beeps after a moment and pulls nearby drones toward it. You get 2 per floor.
  • Mazes get bigger and drones get faster. Getting caught ends the run.
