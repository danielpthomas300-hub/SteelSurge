How to play

  • Your robot's linear slide sweeps a game piece back and forth above the stack.
  • Drop it at the right moment. Anything hanging over the edge of the piece below gets trimmed off, so the tower gets narrower.
  • Line it up almost exactly for a perfect drop: nothing is trimmed, and perfect drops in a row build a combo.
  • The slide speeds up as the tower grows. Miss the stack completely and the run is over.
