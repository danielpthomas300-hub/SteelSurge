How to play

  • Your robot sits on a balancing platform that tips toward whichever side is heavier.
  • Game pieces fall onto the platform. A warning arrow shows where each one will land.
  • Drive over pieces to collect them. Heavier pieces (marked ×2 and ×3) tip the platform more but score more.
  • Use your robot's own weight to keep the platform level. You earn bonus points every second it stays level.
  • Tip too far for too long, or let 3 pieces slide off the ends, and the run is over.
