How to play

  • Real robots use odometry pods, small tracking wheels, to know exactly where they've driven.
  • Watch the robot drive a route across the field. The route is drawn in orange.
  • Then it's your turn: drive the same route from the start pad, one tile at a time.
  • A wrong turn is a strike, and the route plays again. Run out of strikes and the game ends.
  • Each route you finish is one step longer. How long a route can you remember?
