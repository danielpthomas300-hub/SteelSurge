How to play

  • Every robot moves in the same direction at the same time.
  • Robots slide until they hit a wall, the edge, or another robot. Use each other as stoppers.
  • Get every robot onto the pad that matches its color.
  • Finish in par moves for 3 stars. Par is the true minimum; a computer checked every level.
