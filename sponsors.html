Questions

Sponsor FAQ

How do I become a sponsor?

Send us a message through the contact page or email the team. We'll answer your questions, share details, and handle the paperwork with our school and mentors.

Can I donate parts, tools, or services instead of money?

Yes. In-kind support like parts, tools, machining, printing, or a place to build is incredibly helpful. We'll recognize it just like a financial sponsorship.

How will my business be recognized?

Each tier lists its benefits, such as recognition on social media, logo placement on team shirts, and features on this website. We're happy to talk about what works best for you.

Does a sponsorship last all season?

Yes. Sponsorships cover a full FTC season, from the September kickoff through our last event in the spring.
